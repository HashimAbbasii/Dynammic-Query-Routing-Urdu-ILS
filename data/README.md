# data/

Official freeze corpus: **`clean_articles.csv`** (n = 111,860).

Other files in this folder (embeddings, Chroma, `urdu_news.csv`) are **generated or precursor** artifacts. They are gitignored and were **not** deleted in the Stage 2 cleanup. They are not required to read the official M0 metrics.

`training_queries_real.py` is Layer A SVM training data, not M0 routing.
