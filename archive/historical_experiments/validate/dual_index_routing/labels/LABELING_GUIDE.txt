ONE-PAGE LABEL RULE (read this before you label)

What you are deciding
---------------------
SHORT or LONG is NOT "is the query short or long?"
It is: which library ROOM would help a user more?

  Room A = HEADLINES only   -> label SHORT
  Room B = FULL ARTICLES    -> label LONG

Do NOT count words. Do NOT use 150 letters. Do NOT copy the 6-word rule.
If you count words, we can never beat that rule in a paper.

How to choose
-------------
SHORT  The user wants one clear fact (score, price, date, who won).
       A good headline could answer it. Extra story is optional.

LONG   The user wants why / how / how much damage / comparison / reasons.
       A headline like "team lost" is not enough. They need the article.

If you are stuck, leave gold_label BLANK. Do not guess.

Two people (best)
-----------------
Person A and Person B label the SAME sheet independently.
Then we see how often you agree. IEEE likes that number (Cohen's kappa).

Trap types in the sheet (already filled; you do not change these)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
SHORT_WORDS_LONG_NEED  Few words, but the need is a STORY (why/how).
LONG_WORDS_SHORT_NEED  More words, but the need is ONE FACT.
CONTROL_EASY_SHORT     Obvious headline fact.
CONTROL_EASY_LONG      Obvious full-article question.

Fill only: gold_label_A  and  gold_label_B  (SHORT or LONG)
Optional: notes

Person A fills column gold_label_A only.
Person B fills column gold_label_B only. Do not look at A's answers.
