IEEE conference manuscript (IEEEtran, two-column).

Compile on Overleaf: New project → upload this folder → main.tex.

Or locally:
  pdflatex main
  pdflatex main

What you may say at defense
---------------------------
YES: "The paper is written in IEEE conference format and is ready to submit
      to an IEEE venue (e.g. INMIC, ICET, HONET, or IEEE Access after
      expanding it to journal length)."

YES: "We are preparing / we will submit an IEEE paper on this work."

NO:  "I have published in IEEE" / "it is on IEEE Xplore"
     until a venue accepts it and IEEE indexes it.

The numbers in main.tex are the frozen ones (86/84, 60/20/50, P@5 33 vs 36.50).
Do not put development 100% or 96% in the IEEE draft.
